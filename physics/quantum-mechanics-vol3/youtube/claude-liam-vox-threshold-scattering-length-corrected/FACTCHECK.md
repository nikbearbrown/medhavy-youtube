# Factcheck

For low-energy elastic distinguishable-particle s-wave scattering with negligible effective range,
`f_0(k) = -a_s/(1+i k a_s)` and `sigma_0 = 4 pi a_s^2/(1+k^2 a_s^2)`.
Thus `sigma -> 4 pi a_s^2` only when `k|a_s| << 1`; at fixed nonzero `k` and `|a_s| -> infinity`, `sigma -> 4 pi/k^2`, the unitarity limit. A shallow bound state has a large positive scattering length and binding scale approximately `hbar^2/(2 mu a_s^2)` in the universal regime. Crossing threshold produces the scattering-length pole and sign change; the negative side is associated with a virtual state. A Feshbach resonance is a coupled-channel mechanism, commonly parameterized by `a(B)=a_bg[1-Delta/(B-B0)]`.
