# Pedagogy — corrected

The story begins with the impossible classical route, introduces the Bell pair as a resource prepared in advance, walks through the protocol as a visible state transfer, then closes the causality loophole. The body keeps one invariant color law: teal for the intact quantum information and crimson for blocked, destroyed, or scrambled states. The learner's check is to explain why Bob cannot use the qubit before Alice's two bits arrive.
