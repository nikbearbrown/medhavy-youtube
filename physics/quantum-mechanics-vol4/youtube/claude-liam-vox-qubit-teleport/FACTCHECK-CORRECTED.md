# Fact check — corrected

- Quantum teleportation consumes one pre-shared Bell pair and requires two classical bits.
- Alice's Bell-basis measurement destroys her local copy of the unknown state; the protocol does not clone it.
- Bob's conditional state differs from the input by one of the Pauli corrections I, X, Z, or XZ (global-phase conventions may change the written order/sign without changing the physics).
- Before Alice's classical message arrives, Bob's reduced state is maximally mixed and carries no usable information, so the protocol is not faster than light.
- The example `|+>` with outcome `10` assumes the beat sheet's correction-bit convention; applying Z maps `|->` back to `|+>`.

No dense-coding dual or full three-qubit amplitude expansion is used.
