## Central question
Why does measuring spin along x destroy knowledge of spin along z?

## Does the film SET UP THE PROBLEM before answering it?
Yes. B01-B02 establish the counterintuitive result (perpendicular axes seem independent, but they interfere). The answer via non-commutativity arrives in B05.

Yes. The Bloch sphere visualization in B06 — state starting at north pole, projected to equator by x-measurement — carries the intuition that measuring x makes z maximally uncertain. The motion (state moving from pole to equator) IS the explanation.
