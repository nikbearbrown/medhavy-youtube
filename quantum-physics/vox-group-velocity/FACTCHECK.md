# FACTCHECK — vox-group-velocity

## B03 — Phase velocity calculation
- omega = hbar*k^2/(2m) for free particle: CORRECT (E = p^2/2m, omega = E/hbar, k = p/hbar).
- v_ph = omega/k = hbar*k/(2m) = p/(2m) = v/2: CORRECT.
- Phase velocity for non-relativistic de Broglie wave is v/2: CORRECT (standard result, Griffiths QM I.3).

## B04 — Wave packet as superposition
- Localized particle requires superposition of many k-values: CORRECT.
- Plane wave is not localizable: CORRECT.

## B05 — Group velocity derivation
- Sum of two waves with (k, omega) and (k+dk, omega+domega) gives product of carrier and envelope: CORRECT (sum-to-product identity).
- Carrier at average omega/k; envelope at domega/dk: CORRECT.

## B06 — Group velocity equals particle speed
- v_g = domega/dk = hbar*k/m = p/m = v: CORRECT.
- Envelope moves at classical speed v; crests at v/2: CORRECT.

## B07-B08 — Observable implications
- Group velocity is the physically measured particle speed: CORRECT.
- Phase velocity carries no signal or energy: CORRECT (standard QM result).

## B09 — Numerical example
- 100 V electron: v = sqrt(2eV/m) ≈ sqrt(2 * 1.6e-19 / 9.1e-31) ≈ 5.9e6 m/s ≈ 6e6 m/s: CORRECT.
- Phase velocity = v/2 ≈ 3e6 m/s: CORRECT.
- Quadratic dispersion relation omega ∝ k^2 is the source of 2:1 ratio: CORRECT.

VERDICT: All facts verified. The phase velocity = v/2 and group velocity = v result is a textbook standard for non-relativistic de Broglie matter waves. The numerical example is accurate.
