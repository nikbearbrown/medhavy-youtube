# Factcheck

For normalized `|psi_eps>=sqrt(1-eps^2)|0>+eps|1>`, with orthonormal eigenstates, `E[psi_eps]-E0=eps^2(E1-E0)` exactly. This demonstrates stationarity of the normalized Rayleigh quotient and the absence of a linear energy term. A global rescaling cancels after normalization. Norm closeness alone does not uniformly control energy for an unbounded Hamiltonian: small high-energy components can dominate. For a gapped nondegenerate ground state, the total excited-state weight obeys `1-|c0|^2 <= (E-E0)/(E1-E0)`.
