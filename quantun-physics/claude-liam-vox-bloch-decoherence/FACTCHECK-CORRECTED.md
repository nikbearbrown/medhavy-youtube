# Fact check — corrected

The inherited draft conflated pure dephasing, depolarization, and a finite coherence time. This cut applies these corrections:

- The Bloch-ball surface contains all pure states; interior points are mixed; only the center is maximally mixed.
- In the standard pure-dephasing model, environment-branch overlap multiplies the off-diagonal coherence. It does not generally equal the entire Bloch-vector length.
- Pure dephasing shrinks transverse x/y components while leaving populations, and therefore the z component, unchanged. It need not end at the center.
- For exponential decay, T2 is the 1/e timescale. Coherence is about 37 percent at t=T2 and approaches zero asymptotically.
- A quoted 10^-36-second estimate is scoped to a dust grain in air under specific assumptions, not asserted as a universal cat-sized-object constant.
- Decoherence suppresses locally observable interference but does not by itself select a unique measurement outcome.
- A 200-microsecond transmon value is explicitly illustrative and platform-dependent.

No Lindblad derivation, jump-operator formalism, or T1/Tphi algebra is included.
